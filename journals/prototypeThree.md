## 28-10-2026
I have decided that I want to recreate a simple pink terrain that is covered in gems and gemstones. The reason for why I decided to model this is because I was browsing on Pinterest and I was seeing how there is this new trend on like cups, phonecases, makeup products, etc,etc that are covered in gems. Its like this new trend online called whimsymaxxing where you bedazzle every object you have. I was seeing multiple objects while scrolling on Pinterest where there was many gems that was covering it. This stuck to my mind, which is where I then decided to have a terrain full of gems. I would need to find 3D gem models online to implement it on my scene.

I decided for my terrain to model it using the available brushes in Unity. The reason why I wanted to do that is because at first I kinda wanted to do a cave and then when you walk inside it, you find the gems. But I was not able to find a proper 3D model of a cave, as they were all in colors that I didnt prefer. I tried to model the cave myself using the brushes but it was kinda tricky. I then just decided to model with the brushes a mountain, rocky, kind of terrain. No greenery on my terrain. Just mountains, rocky mountains that are pink. The reason why I wanted a pink terrain is purely because I enjoy the color pink, and I think it will suit well the gems that are on top of it. I created a capsule where my camera will be, to do the walking simulation.

## 29-10-2026
I finished completing my terrain. I am satisfying with how I worked with my brushes to create the kind of rocky mountains environnement. I also added a sky and put this blue-green color to mimick a sky that contains auroras. The reason for this approach it to make it seem like my environnement can be from a fictional/fantasy world. Lets say a pink planet that has a rocky surface and has a sky that is full of blue and green auroras. 

The gems I was able to find it in Adobe Stock. It was easy to intergrate the 3D modelled object into my scene. The time consuming part was where I had to place it all around my scene in different scales, directions, positions, rotations, etc. I really wanted my entire terrain to be filled with gems but it was super consuming to do it one by one so I tried my best to cover as much as I can. I also adjuted the colors, as when the 3D model inported to my scene, the color was slightly off compared to Adobe Stock's image. I needed to adjust the lights to make it more like brighter.
