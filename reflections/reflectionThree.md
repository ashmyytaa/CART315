## The Anatomy of Prototypes: Prototypes as Filters, Prototypes as Manifestations of Design Ideas

This reading made me look into perspective into the idea of prototypes. They are not just trials and errors, the unfinished versions before a final result, the backup ideas, etc. Prototypes can be seen as more than that. They are a oppertunity to learn, grow, leanr from mistakes, experiement new things, and to familiarize with the tools, assets, functions, and features.
