# Reading Reflection Two
## CHAPTER 9 -Reflective Conversation with Materials -An interview with Donald Schön by John Bennett

I found this reading really interesting as I learned many new concepts such as reflection in actions and reflection on practice. This reading helped me understand that an initial process regarding a designer's intentions constantly gets changed as they make new moves, as they encounter surprises, new challenges, new understandings and new outcomes. Also, I learned how the initial intention that was put out by the designer can be interpreted in many different ways and can be used in new and different ways. The Scotch tape example was a great example that proved that claim. Other instances can be where a finished product, when we thought was put together by solving any problems and preventing any future problems, can still be presented with problems when published. The Gillette blade example was a relevant one as we got to see how a problem arose as later on, after the product finalization and publication. There is also a conversational relationship between a designer and its design. The medium constantly responds to the choices of the designer and the designer gets to judge, reflect and adjust each response, which can alter the design from its initial variation.


I learned how to design and every designer has their own perspective on what is a good design that is primarily shaped by their personal experience, their emotional experiences, their background, their cultures, etc. We all have our own perspective on things and we all see things differently. Something that is extremely beautiful to me can seem not as much to another person. It shows how there's beauty in everything and to not be afraid to express our fondness for our designs, even if it doesn't speak for everybody else. Having a strong sense of what you like and your preferences will help overcome public opinions, criticism and judgements.
