# Reading Reflection One
## Pippin Barr - The Stuff Games are Made off - Chapter 2

I really liked reading this chapter as it was insightful and nice to read. I have never looked deeper into a game compared to contemporary artwork, as games are seen as something playful, for fun. Which is why I have taken it very light hearted and have never questioned how a game functions, what goes into it. Having to look at games in a different lens, thanks to Pippin's book, made me realize it is by having these conversations and thoughts that you get to understand how all this stuff is really made. Just playing a game is not enough, as it is important to ask all these questions for better play,understanding and connection with the game. A key point that I remembered from chapter 2 is how rules made by a game are entirely malleable, that a game never really is finished due to its constant updates and changes. Rules are malleable, and were all made up by someone.


Seeing the perspective of how in the Pong game, everytime a rule was removed or altered, it created a new perspective and a new way of thinking of the game. The Pong game is so simple, but we can make it as complicated and as simple as we want. I really find it interesting to read between lines and question the game based on its malleability.  It all started with the simple Pong game, but then it ended up being more than that when you get to experience all of its variations. Pippin emphasizes on how it is important to play with the rules and not just to simply go by them. The examples provided at the end also show us different stories and concepts that take on the same fundamentals as the Pong game, with all of its variations.
